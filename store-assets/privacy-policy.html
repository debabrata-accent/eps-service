Privacy Policy — EPS Service

Last updated: 12 September 2026

EPS Service ("we", "our", or "us") operates the EPS Service mobile application and website (the "Service"), a platform for managing electrical panel service tickets for factory owners, customer executives, and specialist engineers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: username, full name, email address, phone number, company name, and role (factory owner, engineer, or customer executive).
  • Service ticket data: factory/site address, contact details, panel information, issue descriptions, and any documents, images, or videos you upload to describe a fault.
  • Payment information: advance payments are processed by our payment provider (Razorpay). We store payment status and transaction references, not your full card or bank details.
  • Device information: for push notifications we store a device messaging token.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage service tickets and user accounts.
  • To process advance payments and confirm them.
  • To notify engineers of available jobs and update owners on service status.
  • To generate reports and maintain an audit trail of ticket activity.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to run the Service: our hosting provider, our database provider (MongoDB Atlas), our file storage provider (Cloudinary), our payment provider (Razorpay), and our push-notification provider (Google Firebase). Ticket details are visible to authorized users within the platform according to their role.

Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (bcrypt). All communication uses HTTPS. Access to data is restricted by role-based authorization.

Data Retention

We retain account and ticket data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. Account deletion requests are honored subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13.
